# Capstone — Enterprise Agile Transformation Command Center
**The crown jewel.** A 90-day simulated enterprise transformation the candidate *leads* — proving they can operate as an AI Enterprise Scrum Master & Agile Transformation Leader, not just facilitate a team.

## Scenario
**Company:** "Northwind Financial" — a 4,000-person bank, 6 product teams in one ART (Agile Release Train), mid-transformation and stalling. You are appointed **RTE / Transformation Lead**. The board has funded a **90-day turnaround** and expects a board presentation at day 90 with measurable outcomes.

**Mandate:** stabilize delivery, restore quality, overcome organizational resistance, and present a credible path to scale — within budget and risk tolerance.

## Structure — 3 sprints × 30 days (decision-driven)
Each "day" advances via decisions; the simulation injects events and scores choices.

### Teams (6) — you coordinate, you don't manage directly
Payments · Onboarding · Lending · Mobile · Data Platform · Risk & Compliance. Each has a maturity level, a hidden impediment, and inter-team dependencies.

### Executive stakeholders (manage all of them)
- **CEO** — wants speed + a board narrative.
- **CTO** — protective of the platform team; skeptical of "Agile theatre."
- **CPO** — pushing scope; weak on prioritization.
- **CFO** — owns the **budget** ($2.4M transformation fund); demands ROI.
- **Head of PMO** — attached to phase gates (the resistance epicenter).
- **Board** — the day-90 audience.

## The three crises (must navigate all)
1. **Delivery crisis (Sprint 1):** a regulator-driven payments release is 3 weeks late; cross-team dependencies are gridlocked. *Decisions:* dependency-break strategy, scope negotiation, transparency to the board.
2. **Quality crisis (Sprint 2):** a production incident traced to a weak Definition of Done across teams; trust drops. *Decisions:* DoD hardening, quality gates vs speed, blameless response.
3. **Organizational resistance (Sprint 3):** the PMO mobilizes against the change; a key team threatens to revert. *Decisions:* coalition-building, executive sponsorship, change strategy (not mandate).

## Levers the candidate controls
- **Budget** — allocate the $2.4M (coaching, tooling, hiring, training) with ROI justification.
- **AI** — deploy the AI co-pilot (delivery analytics, dependency prediction, risk surfacing) — and decide where *not* to trust it.
- **Scaling** — choose the scaling approach (SAFe ART vs LeSS vs team-of-teams) and justify.
- **Risk** — maintain a live risk register; mitigate the regulatory + reputational risks.
- **Delivery** — PI planning, dependency management, flow metrics.

## Deliverables (graded)
1. **90-Day Transformation Plan** (sprint-by-sprint, with success metrics).
2. **Budget allocation + ROI model.**
3. **Risk register** (top 10, mitigations, owners).
4. **Scaling recommendation** (with trade-offs).
5. **AI-enablement plan** (where AI is used, guardrails).
6. **Board presentation** (10-slide deck): situation → actions → measurable outcomes → ask.

## Scoring rubric (100 pts)
| Dimension | Pts | What "excellent" looks like |
|---|:--:|---|
| Systemic diagnosis | 15 | Roots, not symptoms; sees the system |
| Delivery recovery | 15 | Dependencies broken, flow restored, transparent |
| Quality leadership | 12 | Hardened DoD, blameless, speed+quality balanced |
| Change & resistance | 15 | Coalition + sponsorship, not mandate |
| Stakeholder/executive mgmt | 12 | Each exec managed to their motivation |
| Budget & ROI | 10 | Defensible allocation, measurable return |
| AI judgment | 8 | Uses AI well *and* knows its limits |
| Scaling rationale | 8 | Right approach for the context, trade-offs named |
| Board presentation | 5 | Executive-grade clarity and ask |

**Pass = 80+. Distinction = 92+.** Human-reviewed; feeds Placement readiness + Talent Score.

## Why it's the crown jewel
It validates **all 8 competencies at once** (Scrum, Agile, SAFe, Leadership, AI, Metrics, Stakeholders, Scaling) under realistic enterprise pressure — the exact bar an employer hiring a transformation lead would test for.

## Document Body (verbatim)

The following powers remain permanently reserved to the Founder while living:

• Amend the Covenant.

• Amend the Constitution.

• Ratify constitutional manuals.

• Appoint or remove Executive leadership.

• Create or dissolve Institutes.

• Approve institutional strategy.

• Delegate authority.

• Revoke delegated authority.

• Designate a successor in accordance with the governing succession instruments.

These powers shall not be delegated to AI.

These powers shall not be inferred.

These powers shall not be assumed.

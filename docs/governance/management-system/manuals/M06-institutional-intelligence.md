## 0. The Five Questions (Engineering Law)

1. **Why does this capability exist?** So every institutional decision is
   supported by computed evidence, delivered at the moment of decision —
   Covenant Art. II (truth pursued through evidence) and the Measurement
   Principle (unknown is preferable to false certainty).
2. **Which constitutional authority governs it?** Covenant → Constitution →
   Intelligence Architecture (in review) + AIOS design (draft) → AMS →
   this manual; the Launch Decision Principle governs every claim it makes.
3. **Which existing capability does it extend?** The intelligence layer
   (observers, findings, confidence), the briefings engine (5 cadences),
   the cockpit (strip, panels, event bus), the KPI dictionaries on genomes,
   and the CEO agent's reporting — codified into decision-support procedure;
   nothing parallel.
4. **What evidence will prove it works?** Decisions citing computed inputs
   (precedent + intelligence KPIs), briefing currency 100%, early warnings
   reaching the founder within escalation-latency targets, zero fabricated
   values on any executive surface (test-enforced honesty).
5. **How will the Company Brain learn from it?** Every recommendation,
   forecast basis, warning, and strategic review lands in the Brain; decision
   outcomes measured against the intelligence that supported them feed the
   90-day cycle.

possessed") and Art. III (*Excellence* — every dashboard reflecting the
pursuit). Serves every Core System by making their states visible and

**Governs:** executive dashboards, decision intelligence, the KPI framework,
executive reporting, forecasting, early warnings, operational intelligence,
institutional analytics, AI decision assistants, and the strategic review.

## 4. Definitions

**Computed truth** — a value derived from live sources by stated formula;
the only kind permitted on executive surfaces. **Early warning** — a
threshold or state crossing that must reach the founder ahead of cadence.
**Decision intelligence** — the package supporting a decision: computed
state + precedent + confidence-scored recommendation + risk. **AI decision
assistant** — an agent producing decision intelligence; never the decider
(Reserved Powers; Covenant Art. VI).

## 6. Operating Procedures

**P1 — The KPI framework.** Every KPI lives in a dictionary under the Score
Contract: formula · target (or `founder-set pending`) · owner · cadence ·
source — attached to its genome, computed from its source, rendered honestly
(`—` for unmeasured). New KPIs enter via their owning manual's amendment,
never ad-hoc. The framework's registry: the genomes themselves (one home).

**P2 — Executive dashboards.** The cockpit is the primary instrument;
every value computed (FD-2026-007 P3 verified); risk colors only; honesty
markers (measured/posture/unmeasured) mandatory; the 60-Second Rule is the
design law for v2 (gated on AIOS ratification — until then the current
cockpit + Institution Strip serve, and this manual governs their truth).
Dashboard changes ride the UX gate.

**P3 — Executive reporting.** The five cadences (daily/weekly/monthly-board/
quarterly/annual) per the Reporting Bureau: compiled from the same live
sources as the cockpit — *a report may never disagree with the dashboard*;
staleness shows as due; spines per the AIOS design. The CEO agent operates;
the founder reads in 90 seconds (daily) to a year-in-decisions (annual).

**P4 — Early warning systems.** Standing warnings (exhaustive today, extended
by amendment): security gate NO-GO · platform critical · escalation events
(M01 P5 triggers) · Unknown-queue age breach · review-currency breach ·
briefing staleness >48h · charter breach (M05 P7) · KPI target breach where
targets exist. Route: event bus → cockpit red strip → daily brief lead item;
delivery latency is measured (M01 escalation-latency KPI). A warning nobody
defined is a gap; a warning that fired unseen is an incident.

**P5 — Forecasting (honest).** Today's forecasting is trend-based only:
readiness history (Brain-recorded daily) and KPI trajectories — every
forecast states its basis and horizon. Model-based forecasting (enrollment,
revenue, placement) is UNBUILT and stays out of every surface until built,
validated, and ratified — a forecast without a stated basis is a fabricated
number wearing a suit (Measurement Principle).

**P6 — Operational intelligence.** The intelligence layer per its ratified
discipline: observers over live telemetry → evidence-gated findings →
confidence-with-basis → deduplicated recommendations into governance.
Sweeps founder-triggered until M05 P9 preconditions; external intelligence
remains NOT CONNECTED until the Research Handbook ratifies sources (the
standing banner stays).

**P7 — AI decision assistants.** Assistants (CEO agent's briefs and
recommendations, intelligence findings, per-department analyses) produce
decision intelligence packages: computed state + Brain precedent +
confidence(+basis) + risks. Constitutional boundary (Reserved Powers,
verbatim): assistants **shall not decide** — they inform the decider; the
recommendation's confidence never substitutes for the founder's evidence
review. Every package is Brain-recorded so decision outcomes can be measured
against the intelligence that supported them (P8's raw material).

**P8 — The strategic review process.** Quarterly, founder-led, compiled by
analytics-intelligence: score movements with causes · top recommendations by
confidence · dimension trends · canon reviews due · decision-outcome
retrospective (which intelligence proved right/wrong — feeding assistant
improvement) · roadmap proposals as draft work orders. Output ratified into
the Brain as the quarterly record; misses become lessons, not embarrassments
(Covenant Art. II: intellectually honest when evidence challenges beliefs).

**P9 — Evidence-based decision making (the binding synthesis).** Every
founder decision of consequence receives: the computed state (P1–P2),
precedent (M04 P5), the recommendation with confidence (P7), and the risk
picture (P4). If any element is unavailable, the package says so — an absent
input stated is decision support; an absent input papered over is sabotage.

## 7. Quality Gates & Standards

No manual assertion on any executive surface (verified: computed-truth audit,
FD-2026-007) · every score carries basis markers · every forecast states
basis + horizon · every warning has an owner and a route · every
recommendation carries confidence WITH basis (validateFinding /
validateRecommendation — enforced) · every strategic review cites its data.
Standard: **the Institution never knows things it cannot show.**

## 11. Risk & Escalation Model

| Risk | L/I | Control | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fabricated certainty (the cardinal risk) | low (enforced) / critical | computed-truth gates + basis markers + P5's forecast rule | any instance = critical incident → founder |
| Warning fatigue (too many reds) | medium/medium | warnings are defined, owned, threshold-reviewed at 90 days | strategic review |
| Assistant over-reach (recommendation treated as decision) | low/high | P7 constitutional boundary + Reserved Powers verbatim | charter breach → M05 P7 |
| Intelligence blind spots (unmeasured domains) | current/medium | honesty markers make blindness visible; gap list standing | quarterly review priorities |
| Cockpit v2 pressure before AIOS ratification | medium/low | P2 gate respected; current cockpit governed meanwhile | founder ratification path |

## 12. Company Brain Integration

Writes: recommendations + outcomes, warnings fired, forecast bases, strategic
reviews, decision-intelligence packages. Reads: precedent for every package
(P9); prior recommendation outcomes calibrate assistant confidence (the
Institution learns which of its advisors to trust, with evidence). The
retrospective rule: intelligence that proved wrong is recorded as diligently
as intelligence that proved right.

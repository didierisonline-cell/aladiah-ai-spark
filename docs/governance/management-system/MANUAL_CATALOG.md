## Sequencing note (for Founder review)

The catalog is written to begin with **M01 — Executive Office Manual**, per
the directive's closing line. Recommended authoring order thereafter follows
dependency, not number: M02–M03 (the machinery that governs the rest), M07–M08
(how work moves), then domain manuals as their departments mature. Order is
the Founder's call.

## Boundary declarations

- **M01 vs. Founding Library shelf 14** (Founder Operating Manual): M01
  governs the *office*; shelf 14 is the Founder's *personal* operating
  doctrine, founder-authored under library rules. They reference, never
  duplicate.
- **Manuals vs. Charters**: charters say what a department *is*; manuals say
  how its work *runs*. A conflict is a governance finding.
- No manual may cover the Shadow Curriculum Factory as an operating
  procedure — its 37 Unknowns resolve through the founder walk first.

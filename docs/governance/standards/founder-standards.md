# Founder Standards

**Status: RESERVED — v0.0 structural scaffold. This document has no content by
design: the Founder Standards are authored by the founder, never invented by
an agent.** Registry key: `founder-standards`.

The Founder Standards will be the operational interpretation of the
Constitution — how every decision is made. Per the AIOS design, it should be
**compiled from precedent** (the Company Brain's `founder-decision` and
`governance-record` entries), not written from imagination.

## Reserved structure (to be filled by the founder)

1. **The evidence bar** — what proof each decision class requires.
2. **Delegation** — what may be decided without the founder, by whom.
3. **Time-boxes** — how long anything may sit in review.
4. **The writing standard** — honest metrics, plain language, no fabrication.
5. **The definition of done** — walked live, measured, remembered.
6. **Escalation** — when and how anything reaches the founder immediately.

## Compliance hook

Every future governing document must declare compliance with this document
once it is ratified (registry `dependencies` must include
`founder-standards`). Until ratification, the ratified canon
(`LAUNCH_DECISION_PRINCIPLE.md`) governs decisions directly.

## Binding consequences

1. **Single focus.** Program production work targets exactly one flagship at a
   time, in the order above. Work on a later program does not begin until the
   current flagship is complete by the ratified gates (QA_STANDARD DoD +
   PROGRAM_VALIDATION_GATE).
2. **The benchmark ratchet.** Each completed flagship sets the institutional
   quality floor. A subsequent program is measured against the full capability
   set of its predecessor — comparison never proceeds by lowering the bar.
3. **Capability inheritance.** Platform capabilities built for a flagship
   (AVIS visuals, tutor support, quiz feedback, simulations, portfolio
   artifacts, and their successors) are institutional, not program-local: the
   next flagship inherits all of them and adds discipline-specific ones.
4. **Platform evolution path.** The Aladiah Platform evolves through flagship
   programs; every completed flagship improves the entire Institution. Platform
   work orders are prioritized by what the current flagship needs.

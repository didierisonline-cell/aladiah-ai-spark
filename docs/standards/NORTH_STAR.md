# NORTH_STAR.md — Aladiah Execution North Star

**Do not break current momentum.**

## Mission

Aladiah will not compete to become the biggest online school. Aladiah will compete to
become the **most effective AI-powered career-transformation platform in the world.**

The goal is not course completion. **The goal is career transformation.**

Every decision must improve at least one of: student competency · student employability ·
student income · student career growth · employer trust · placement success.

## Rule 1 — Do not interrupt current development flow

Active foundation priorities (no new feature may derail these):

1. Finish Module 1 quality upgrade.
2. Complete competency taxonomy implementation.
3. Complete quiz tagging architecture.
4. Complete simulation framework foundation.
5. Complete AI Professor Didier foundation.
6. Complete student competency tracking foundation.

## Rule 2 — Build around outcomes, not content

Traditional model: Video → Quiz → Certificate.
Aladiah model: **Learn → Practice → Simulate → Validate → Interview → Place → Grow.**

## Rule 3 — Every program contains five pillars

- **Pillar 1 — World-Class Content.** Top-5% quality: structured curriculum,
  competency-based progression, mastery learning, real examples, practical exercises.
- **Pillar 2 — AI Tutor.** Every student gets an AI mentor / tutor / coach / study
  assistant / career advisor, 24/7.
- **Pillar 3 — Simulation Engine** *(biggest differentiator)*. Crisis, stakeholder,
  leadership, communication, and real-world business simulations. Target: 100+ per program.
- **Pillar 4 — Competency Engine.** Measurable scores for knowledge, communication,
  leadership, decision making, collaboration, problem solving. Certificates alone are not enough.
- **Pillar 5 — Career Transformation Engine.** Resume optimization, LinkedIn optimization,
  interview prep, portfolio building, employer matching, placement support.

## Rule 4 — Content is not our moat. Transformation is

Do not beat Coursera on content volume. Beat them on AI tutoring, simulations,
personalization, coaching, accountability, placement, and career outcomes.

## Rule 5 — Build the Aladiah Student Profile

Long-term: employers should care more about an Aladiah Profile than a certificate.
Profile contains: competency scores · simulation history · interview scores · project
history · leadership scores · communication scores · employer-readiness score.

## Rule 6 — Measure what matters

Track job-placement rate, salary growth, promotion rate, interview-success rate, student
retention, employer satisfaction. Do not optimize for course completion alone.

## Rule 7 — Africa + Caribbean first

Expansion sequence: Dominican Republic → Cameroon → Ghana → Nigeria → Senegal → Kenya.
Build the dominant AI career ecosystem for emerging markets before going global.

## Rule 8 — The quarterly question

"Does this feature make students more employable?" No → deprioritize. Yes → accelerate.

## Success definition

The goal is not to build an academy. It is to build the world's most trusted AI-powered
talent-transformation ecosystem. When employers start saying **"Show me their Aladiah
profile,"** we have won.

## Who "Prof. Didier" is

The founder is **Didier**. The brand mentor **"Prof. Didier"** (the AI instructor
across the platform) is the founder's teaching persona. "The Man Who Refused" is
Didier's own journey — which is also the origin of Prof. Didier. Keep the label
**"Prof. Didier"** verbatim (protected term).

## The through-line: *refusal*

The series title is **"The Man Who Refused."** The spine of every asset is a
refusal that maps to the mission:

- Refused to accept that where you're born decides what you become.
- Refused to accept that talent in Africa and Latin America should go unrealized.
- Refused to accept education that is too slow, too expensive, too disconnected
  from real jobs.
- Refused to wait for the future of work — chose to build the on-ramp to it.

## Voice & tone

- **Dignified, not desperate.** Aspirational, pan-African and pan-Latin-American
  empowerment. Speaks *to* ambition, never down to hardship.
- **Plain and direct.** Short sentences. No corporate filler.
- **Truthful.** We are a Truth-canon company. No hype we can't stand behind.
- Mentor energy: Prof. Didier is a guide, not a guru.

## Hard guardrails (QA fails the asset if violated)

1. **No invented biography.** Only the facts table above. No invented dates,
   schools, siblings, employers, diplomatic postings, or anecdotes presented as
   fact. Emotional narrative is allowed; *new facts* are not.
2. **No fabricated metrics.** Any number — salary, success rate, student count,
   time-to-job, partner name/logo — must be written as `[VERIFY: …]` until the
   founder supplies a real, defensible figure. Aligns with the Truth Dashboard
   canon (RevenueTruth / EmploymentOutcomes).
3. **Protected terms verbatim:** Aladiah Academy, Prof. Didier, Talent Score™,
   All-Access Pass™ (see `docs/i18n/PROTECTED_TERMS.md`).
4. **Do not publish.** Everything is a draft at `pending_approval` for founder
   review (Marketing Content Agent: `publish:false`, `human_approval_required`).

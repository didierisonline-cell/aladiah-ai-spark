## CAROUSEL 05
**Title:** "The Honest Guide to Scrum: What It Is and What It Is Not"
**Pillar:** AI Careers | **Flywheel:** Trust

**SLIDE 1 — Cover**
> The Honest Guide to Scrum
> What most people get wrong — and why it matters.
> *Swipe →*

**SLIDE 2**
> Scrum is NOT:
> A project plan
> A gantt chart
> A management style
> A way to do more meetings

**SLIDE 3**
> Scrum IS:
> A framework for delivering complex work
> in short, focused cycles called Sprints
> with a team that self-organizes around shared goals.

**SLIDE 4**
> The 3 Scrum Roles
>
> Product Owner: What do we build?
> Scrum Master: How do we build it well?
> Development Team: We build it.

**SLIDE 5**
> The 5 Scrum Events
>
> Sprint Planning · Daily Scrum · Sprint Review
> Sprint Retrospective · The Sprint itself
>
> Each event has a purpose. Skipping one breaks the system.

**SLIDE 6**
> The 3 Scrum Artifacts
>
> Product Backlog: Everything we might build.
> Sprint Backlog: What we committed to this Sprint.
> Increment: What we shipped.

**SLIDE 7**
> Why it matters in the AI era:
>
> AI generates outputs faster than teams can evaluate them.
>
> Scrum provides the structure that ensures AI outputs become real value —
> not just features that nobody uses.

**2 — Why traditional education is breaking**

Traditional education has three problems, and most people feel all three:

→ Too slow. The market moves in months; degrees take years.
→ Too expensive. You can finish with debt and still not have the job.
→ Too disconnected. The thing you studied isn't the thing employers pay for.

The fix isn't to burn it all down. It's to measure education by a different
number: **transformation**, not time served.

Did you walk in unable to do the work, and walk out able to prove you can? That's
the only metric that matters.

That's what we optimize for at Aladiah Academy.

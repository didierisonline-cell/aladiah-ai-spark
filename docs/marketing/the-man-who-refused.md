## Episode 1 — Cameroon
**Title card:** *The Man Who Refused — Ep.1: Cameroon*

**Script (VO):**
He was born in Denver. He was raised in Cameroon. A diplomat's son — which meant
he learned early that the world is bigger than the place you stand in.
In Cameroon he saw something he would never forget: brilliance everywhere, and
doors almost nowhere. Kids who could out-think anyone, with no on-ramp to prove it.
That's where the refusal began. He refused to believe that talent and opportunity
live on the same map. One is everywhere. The other is rationed. He decided, even
then, that he would spend his life closing that gap.

**On-screen text:** Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not. · *Aladiah Academy*
**CTA:** Follow the story. → Ep.2: France.
**B-roll:** Cameroon streets/markets, classrooms, a child reading. **Audio:** warm, rising.

## Episode 2 — France
**Title card:** *Ep.2: France*

**Script (VO):**
France taught him a second language and a hard lesson. The system was old,
beautiful, and slow. Degrees took years. They cost fortunes. And at the end of
them, too many graduates still couldn't get the job the degree promised.
He started asking the question that would become a company: *What if education
was measured by transformation — not by time served?*
He refused to accept that "prestigious" should mean "slow and expensive."

**On-screen text:** Education should be measured in transformation — not years.
**CTA:** → Ep.3: Washington, DC.
**B-roll:** Paris, lecture halls, clocks, tuition paperwork. **Audio:** reflective.

## Episode 3 — Washington, DC
**Title card:** *Ep.3: Washington, DC*

**Script (VO):**
Washington is a city of power and credentials. He watched rooms where the smartest
person and the most-hired person were almost never the same person.
The difference wasn't intelligence. It was access — the right network, the right
signal, the right paper.
He refused to accept that your career should depend on who you already know. He
started imagining a school that hands you the *signal* you earned — not the one
you were born near.

**On-screen text:** Your work should be the credential.
**CTA:** → Ep.4: Miami.
**B-roll:** DC skyline, offices, handshake culture. **Audio:** tense, purposeful.

## Episode 4 — Miami
**Title card:** *Ep.4: Miami*

**Script (VO):**
Miami is where the Americas meet. Spanish and English in the same sentence.
Ambition from every country, in one city.
Here the vision sharpened: this isn't a Cameroon problem, or a France problem. It's
a *global workforce* problem — and the next decade would be defined by one thing.
AI. The question wasn't whether AI would change every job. It was who would get to
ride that wave — and who would get left explaining why they didn't.
He refused to let the AI future be something that happens *to* people instead of
*for* them.

**On-screen text:** AI shouldn't happen *to* you. It should work *for* you.
**CTA:** → Ep.5: Dominican Republic.
**B-roll:** Miami, bilingual signage, skyline, tech. **Audio:** bright, modern.
